# Dutch Legal-Protection Regimes (CBM): Factual Report for Bewindvoering Software

**Scope:** curatele / beschermingsbewind / mentorschap — duties, deadlines, quality rules, tariffs, digital filing, market size. Compiled 2026-08-25 from primary sources (wetten.overheid.nl, the LOVT Aanbevelingen April 2025 PDF, rechtspraak.nl, rijksoverheid.nl) and sector sources.

---

## 1. The three regimes (Boek 1 BW)

| Regime | Covers | Legal basis | Effect on client (betrokkene) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Curatele** (full guardianship) | Financial **and** personal interests | Boek 1 BW, Titel 16, art. 1:378–391 | Client becomes **handelingsonbekwaam** (legally incompetent); curator is full legal representative |
| **Beschermingsbewind** (protective administration of property) | Money and goods (all assets, or specific assets only — "beperkt bewind") | Boek 1 BW, Titel 19, art. 1:431–449 | Client stays legally competent but **cannot dispose of assets under bewind** without the bewindvoerder |
| **Mentorschap** (mentorship/personal guardianship) | Non-financial personal interests: **verzorging, verpleging, behandeling, begeleiding** (care, nursing, treatment, guidance) | Boek 1 BW, Titel 20, art. 1:450–462 | Client is handelingsonbevoegd for care decisions covered by the mentorschap |

- Grounds for bewind (art. 1:431 BW): (a) lichamelijke of geestelijke toestand (physical/mental condition), or (b) **verkwisting of problematische schulden** (prodigality or problematic debts). "Problematische schulden" = foreseeably unable to keep paying debts (LOVT B.A8) — this ground drives the **schuldenbewind** market and the higher tariff.
- Bewinden on the debt/prodigality ground are **always published** in the **Centraal curatele- en bewindregister (CCBR)** (LOVT B.A9); the register was renamed/extended per 1 Jan (CCBR) with public schuldenbewind lookup from 1 April ([NJB](https://www.njb.nl/nieuws/nieuw-register-voor-curatele-en-bewind/), [Rijksoverheid CCBR](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/wetten-en-regelingen/productbeschrijvingen/inzage-centraal-curatele--en-bewindregister)).
- One person can combine bewind + mentorschap; curatele is the "heaviest" measure used only when the lighter combination is insufficient ([Rijksoverheid overview](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/curatele-bewind-en-mentorschap/vraag-en-antwoord/verschillen-curatele-beschermingsbewind-en-mentorschap)).
- A "professionele uitvoerder" = anyone acting for **3 or more** clients (LOVT L.A1; art. 1:435 lid 7 BW) — this triggers the whole quality regime in §3.

**Framework law update:** the Wet wijziging curatele, beschermingsbewind en mentorschap (dossier 33054, in force 2014) added the debt ground, quality requirements, and the 5-yearly evaluation ([Eerste Kamer](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/33054_wet_wijziging_curatele)).

## 2. Statutory & court-imposed duties with deadlines

Authoritative operational source: **LOVT Aanbevelingen Meerderjarigenbewind, Curatele en Mentorschap, adopted 3 April 2025** (consolidated the three previous documents into one; supersedes the 2023/2021/2019 versions) — [PDF](https://bijstand.dewijsmaker.nl/system/files/2025-04/Aanbevelingen-CBM-vastgesteld-LOVT-3-april-2025.pdf), [Rechtspraak news](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/organisatie-en-contact/organisatie/raad-voor-de-rechtspraak/nieuws/2025/04/aanbevelingen-curatele-bewind-en-mentorschap-geactualiseerd).

### Timeline of obligations per dossier

| Obligation | Deadline | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Boedelbeschrijving** (inventory of assets) | Within **4 months** of start of bewind; signed by bewindvoerder (+ client if capable) | Art. 1:436 BW; LOVT B.B1 |
| **Plan van aanpak** (plan of approach; mandatory for professionals, plus a supplementary debt plan for schuldenbewind) | With the petition; at latest together with the boedelbeschrijving; updated after dossier takeovers | LOVT B.B4; art. 1:432a BW (court may demand copies within 3 months) |
| Open **beheerrekening + leefgeldrekening** in client's name, notify banks, block cards | Immediately after appointment | LOVT B.B2 |
| Register bewind in **Kadaster** (if real estate) and **Handelsregister** (if business) | On discovery | LOVT B.B8 |
| **Rekening en verantwoording (R&V)** — annual accounting to the kantonrechter | **Annually** (art. 1:445 lid 1 BW); court sets the exact schedule per dossier (usually anniversary date or calendar year) | Art. 1:445 BW; LOVT B.B11–B.B13 |
| **5-yearly evaluation** (periodiek verslag) — report on course of the measure and **whether it should continue** or can be lightened | At least **once per 5 years**, or sooner if court demands; in an evaluation year no separate annual verslag needed (mentorschap) | Art. 1:446a BW (bewind), art. 1:459a BW (mentorschap); LOVT M.B10 |
| **Eindrekening en verantwoording** after opheffing (lifting) of bewind | Within **2 months** after end of bewind | LOVT B.N2 |
| Eindrekening after **death of client** | Within **4 months** after death, signed by heirs/executor (verklaring van erfrecht required, waivable), then filed with kantonrechter | LOVT B.O2; art. 1:445 lid 5 jo. 1:373 BW |
| Accept/reject inheritance decisions | 3-month regime of art. 4:193 BW; **beneficiaire aanvaarding mandatory** if client wilsonbekwaam | LOVT B.K1–B.K2 |

### R&V content requirements (LOVT B.B11–B.B13, B.C1)
- Covers **all assets under bewind**; income, expenses, opening/closing balances, comparisons, totals and summary matching the rechtspraak.nl model form ([form PDF](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/SiteCollectionDocuments/rekening-en-verantwoording.pdf), [all forms](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/onderwerpen/bewind/alle-formulieren-voor-bewindvoering)). Own templates allowed **only** if they contain the same line items/totals.
- **Leefgeldrekening: only opening and closing balance** — individual leefgeld spending need not be justified (major workload relief; B.B11).
- Attach bank statements/annual overviews for **every account**, exported from the bank's system with bank logo — **screenshots of a banking app are not accepted** (B.B12).
- PGB verlenings- en vaststellingsbeschikkingen must be attached annually (B.C1).
- Client signs "voor akkoord" if capable of understanding it, else "voor gezien"; discussion of the R&V should happen at a **physical annual contact moment** (B.B13).
- Evidence of client/court consent for €2,000+ expenditures must be attached (B.D3).

### Machtiging (prior court authorization) — art. 1:441 lid 2 BW
Statutory list (toestemming of client if wilsbekwaam, otherwise machtiging kantonrechter) — [text](https://wetboekplus.nl/burgerlijk-wetboek-boek-1-artikel-441-taak-bewindvoerder/):
a. **beschikken** (disposing) over assets under bewind, unless normal management;
b. accepting gifts/bequests with conditions or burdens;
c. **borrowing money** or committing the client as guarantor/co-debtor;
d. agreements to keep an estate undivided;
e. **vaststellingsovereenkomsten** (settlement agreements), except where dispute value ≤ **€700**;
f. other acts designated by the court.

LOVT 2025 operationalization (section B.D and B.J):
- **⚠️ Threshold changed: purchases/sales of goods above €2,000** now require toestemming/machtiging (B.D2). **Older sources and many kanton court pages still cite €1,500** — the April 2025 Aanbevelingen raised it. Multiple same-purpose expenditures in a year (driving lessons, holidays, furnishing a home) are **summed** toward the €2,000 (B.D3).
- Sale/purchase of a **woning** (home), (ending) lease agreements, all lending/borrowing, **all schenkingen** (gifts): always machtiging if client not wilsbekwaam (B.D2). Exception for lease termination when moving to equivalent/cheaper housing or permanently to a care institution (B.D5).
- **Schenkingen**: only allowed with proven **schenkingstraditie**, and liquid assets may not drop below **€30,000** after the gift; putting client assets into a BV/stichting is prohibited (HR 20-11-2015, ECLI:NL:HR:2015:3334) (B.J1).
- Investing only within **defensief/zeer defensief profile** (NVB risk scales 1–3); riskier profiles, hiring a vermogensbeheerder: machtiging. **Crypto prohibited** (B.D4).
- No loans/advances from bewindvoerder to client without prior machtiging (B.B7); no self-granted extra assignments (art. 9 Besluit kwaliteitseisen; B.B14).
- Extra remuneration (extra hours, outsourced bookkeeping, estate settlement beyond normal scope) always requires **prior machtiging** (B.H5, B.H9, B.K8).
- >€1m vermogen: 0.75% fee only via court assessment (B.H7). >€100,000 cash must be spread across banks under the DNB deposit-guarantee (B.B2).
- Checklist for machtiging requests: [rechtspraak.nl checklist](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/Onderwerpen/paginas/checklist-machtigingsverzoek.aspx), [machtigingsverzoek](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/Onderwerpen/Paginas/Machtigingsverzoek.aspx).

For **curatele** the machtiging regime of minor-guardianship applies (art. 1:386 jo. 1:345 BW) plus the bewind aanbevelingen by analogy (LOVT C.B4).

## 3. Quality requirements — Besluit kwaliteitseisen + Landelijk Kwaliteitsbureau CBM

**Besluit kwaliteitseisen curatoren, beschermingsbewindvoerders en mentoren** ([BWBR0034760](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0034760); amended Stb. 2021, 469):

- **Education (art. 3):** bewindvoerders/curatoren need a relevant **hbo** qualification, or mbo-4 plus ≥2 years supervised practice (≥24 h/wk); annual **permanente educatie** required. EVC route accepted only with a full diploma (LOVT L.A5). Whether a degree is "passend" is for the kantonrechter (L.A4).
- **VOG** (certificate of conduct) with prescribed screening profiles (12, 21, 22, 43, 53, 85 for bewindvoerders) for **every person** who performs tasks (LOVT L.A3).
- **Accessibility & contact (art. 5):** reachable by phone ≥4 working days/week, respond within 2 working days; periodic in-person contact; **monthly account overview to the client** unless declined/pointless (art. 5 lid 6) — a core software feature.
- **Klachtenregeling (art. 6):** written complaints procedure; resolution within **6 weeks**; handled by someone other than the person complained about where possible; kantonrechter is the escalation route (LOVT section P).
- **Dossier requirements (art. 7):** complete file per client incl. finance/care documents and logged contact attempts.
- **Separation of duties (art. 8 lid 4):** payment authorization separated from payment execution and bookkeeping (exemption for sole operators).
- **Integrity (art. 9):** no gifts, no buying client goods, no benefiting via related parties, no side income from a dossier.
- **Annual accountant review (art. 11):** yearly submission to the kantonrechter/LKB of: compliance checklist + evidence, **rapport inzake overeengekomen specifieke werkzaamheden** (agreed-upon procedures report on file compliance — sample of min. 10 files or 10%, max 100, art. 11 lid 2), annual accounts/balans + staat van baten en lasten, and the accountant's **controleverklaring** (or **samenstellingsverklaring** for small organizations meeting art. 2:396 BW criteria) — annual **Accountantsprotocol** published via the NBA ([NBA protocol page](https://www.nba.nl/wet--en-regelgeving/controleprotocollen/ministeries/justitie-en-veiligheid/cbm/); LOVT L.A2). Mentors may substitute a court-appointed expert (art. 12).
- **Bank accounts:** the Aanbevelingen (B.B2) require **two accounts in the client's own name** — a **beheerrekening** (management account, sole control noted with the bank) and a **leefgeldrekening** (living-allowance account: no credit facility, only bewindvoerder may deposit, third-party deposits must be policed). Accounts "bewindvoerder inzake client", en/of accounts, tussenrekeningen and derdengeldenrekeningen are **prohibited**, even briefly.
- **Caseload:** no hard statutory cap for bewind; courts state ±**60–80 regular dossiers per fte** (benchmark 70) as the max without support ([Kamerstuk 36464 initiatiefnota](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36464-2.html)). For volunteer mentors: max **4 mentorschappen per volunteer**, coordinator max **120 mentorschappen per fte** (LOVT L.A6).

**Landelijk Kwaliteitsbureau CBM (LKB)** — at Rechtbank Oost-Brabant, screens all professional CBM'ers **on behalf of the kantonrechters** since 1-1-2016 ([LKB page](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/voor-advocaten-en-juristen/reglementen-procedures-en-formulieren/civiel/curatele-bewind-en-mentorschap/landelijk-kwaliteitsbureau-cbm)):
- **Toelatingsverzoek** (admission request) at entry; thereafter an annual **handhavingsverzoek** (maintenance request) proving continued compliance; the annual quality-document window runs roughly **April–October**, individual due dates shown in the portal with a 1-month reminder; ~16-week processing target ([FAQ](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/voor-advocaten-en-juristen/reglementen-procedures-en-formulieren/civiel/curatele-bewind-en-mentorschap/landelijk-kwaliteitsbureau-cbm/veelgestelde-vragen)).
- Since 4 March 2024: submissions via the web portal **Mijn Rechtspraak – Landelijk Kwaliteitsbureau CBM ("Mijn LKB")**, incl. new-employee approvals ([werkinstructie PDF](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/binaries/_rts_1755101914163/content/assets/ivo/wi/werkinstructie-handhavingsverzoek-indienen-via-mijn-rechtspraak-lkb-cbm.pdf), [NVVK](https://www.nvvk.nl/nieuws-detail/2024/03/04/Rechtspraak-vergemakkelijkt-digitaal-contact)).
- LKB only tests quality compliance; the **local kantonrechter** still decides appointments per case (LOVT L.A1). Bulk portfolio transfers between offices need prior contact with kantonrechter **and** LKB (B.E4).

## 4. Remuneration — Regeling beloning curatoren, bewindvoerders en mentoren ([BWBR0035730](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0035730), 2026 version effective 1-1-2026; indexation notice [Stcrt. 2025, 39037](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-39037.html))

All professional amounts are **per year, incl. onkostenvergoeding, excl. BTW (21%)**. Forfaitair (flat-fee) system: standard bewind is normed at ~**17 hours/year** of work.

**Professional bewindvoerder (art. 3), single client:**

| Item | 2025 | **2026** |
|---|---|---|
| Standard bewind | ±€1,403/yr (€116.91/mo excl BTW; €141.47 incl) | **€1,630/yr** (≈€135.83/mo excl BTW) |
| Schuldenbewind (problematic debts) | ±€1,814/yr (€151.16/mo excl; €182.91 incl) | **€2,107/yr** (≈€175.58/mo excl BTW) |
| Aanvangswerkzaamheden (intake) | €751 (2025 approx.) | **€767** (€574 if preceded by own budgetbeheer) |
| Verhuizing/ontruiming/verkoop woning | — | **€478** per event |
| Beheer PGB | — | **€718**/yr |
| Eindrekening en verantwoording | — | **€288** one-off |
| Vermogen > €1 mln | 0.75% of assets (court-assessed, extra work must be shown — LOVT B.H7) | same |

- **Professional curator (art. 2):** standard **€2,933**; with schulden or young-adult (18–23, ex-jeugdzorg) surcharge **€3,361**; both **€3,794**; intake €1,380; eindrekening €288. **Professional mentor (art. 4):** €1,630; young-adult €2,107; intake €767.
- Two-person households (arts. 6–8): bewind €1,954–€2,528; curatele €4,235–€5,481. Combined measures (arts. 5, 9, 10) have their own tables.
- **Family/non-professional (art. 1):** bewindvoerder or mentor **€883**/yr (2025: €760), curator **€1,593** (2025: €1,371) — granted on request via approval of the R&V (LOVT B.H2).
- **Extra work:** only for "uitzonderlijke werkzaamheden", **prior machtiging required**, at an hourly rate — sector sources quote **€95.90/hr for 2026** ([normbedragen.nl](https://normbedragen.nl/rekening-en-verantwoording-bewindvoerder-pdf/vergoeding-bewindvoerder/), [beheersjebudget.nl](https://beheersjebudget.nl/tarieven-bewindvoering/)); verify against Stcrt. 2025, 39037 — the consolidated wetten.nl text keeps hour-based beloning in the exception clauses. Ongoing tax returns, routine estate settlement and normal admin fall **inside** the flat fee (B.H5, B.H9, B.K7).
- **2026 change to flag:** fees rose unusually hard — **5.1629% indexation plus a one-off +10.5% structural increase** (following the 2023 WODC/kostprijs discussion that bewind actually takes ~27 h/yr vs 17 funded — [Kamerstuk 36464](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36464-2.html)); kantonrechters publicly urged further increases in 2026 ([NVVK, May 2026](https://www.nvvk.nl/nieuws-detail/2026/05/11/rechters-verhoog-beloning-bewindvoerders)).
- **Who pays:** the client, deducted monthly from the beheerrekening. Clients with income up to ±120% of the bijstandsnorm get **bijzondere bijstand** from the gemeente (must be applied for; must be paid **directly onto the beheerrekening** per LOVT B.B10; typical application windows ~8 weeks after invoice) — [Gemeente Haarlem](https://haarlem.nl/vergoeding-kosten-bewindvoering), [Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/curatele-bewind-en-mentorschap/vraag-en-antwoord/financiele-vergoeding-curator-beschermingsbewindvoerder-of-mentor). A large share of schuldenbewind clients is bijstand-funded, so gemeenten are de facto payers.
- Beloning accrues **pro rata per day** from the day after the benoemingsbeschikking (B.H6); schuldenbewind tariff must be **self-downgraded** to the standard tariff the month after debts are cleared/schone lei (B.I2).

**Business-thesis confirmation:** revenue per standard dossier is capped at €1,630/yr (2026) for ~17 normed hours; extra billing requires case-by-case court machtiging. Margin therefore comes almost entirely from cost-per-dossier efficiency — exactly the wedge for software.

## 5. Digital submission

- **Mandatory from 1 January 2026:** professional curatoren/bewindvoerders/mentoren **must** communicate electronically with the Rechtspraak (Besluit verplicht elektronisch communiceren professionele wettelijke vertegenwoordigers, Stb. 30-12-2024) — [Rechtspraak news](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/organisatie-en-contact/organisatie/raad-voor-de-rechtspraak/nieuws/vanaf-2026-verplicht-elektronische-communiceren-voor-professionele-wettelijke-vertegenwoordigers), [NVVK explainer](https://www.nvvk.nl/nieuws-detail/2025/03/03/verplichte-elektronische-communicatie-voor-bewindvoerders-wat-verandert-er). Two channels ([digitaal toezicht page](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/voor-advocaten-en-juristen/reglementen-procedures-en-formulieren/civiel/curatele-bewind-en-mentorschap/digitaal-toezicht)):
  1. **Aansluitpunt Toezicht** — system-to-system interface between the courts' Toezicht system and the professional's own software. **This is the integration a bewindvoerdersoftware product must build**; existing vendors (e.g. OnView/Estrategy ecosystem) already connect ([Estrategy](https://www.estrategy.nl/software-oplossingen/rechtspraak/)).
  2. **Mijn CBM** (= Mijn Rechtspraak – Curatele, Bewind en Mentorschap) — secure web portal fallback for offices without a connected vendor; supports entering R&V with live totals, filing machtigingsverzoeken, boedelbeschrijvingen, etc.
- Family (particuliere) representatives may still file on paper using the rechtspraak.nl model forms ([forms overview](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/onderwerpen/bewind/alle-formulieren-voor-bewindvoering); LOVT B.B3).
- **LKB stream is separate:** annual handhavingsverzoek + quality documents (accountant reports, checklist) go through **Mijn Rechtspraak – LKB** (since March 2024), not through Toezicht/Mijn CBM.
- So a professional agency runs three recurring filing flows: (1) per-dossier supervision filings (boedelbeschrijving, plan van aanpak, R&V, 5-year evaluation, machtigingen, eindrekening) via Aansluitpunt/Mijn CBM; (2) annual office-level LKB handhavingsverzoek with accountant deliverables; (3) per-client bijzondere-bijstand applications to gemeenten.

## 6. Market size & caseload

- **±254,000 people under beschermingsbewind** currently cited (2024) ([Zelf Magazine](https://www.zelfmagazine.nl/artikelen/in-nederland-staan-momenteel-254-000-mensen-onder-bewind/)); research count **273,000 on 1-1-2022**, of which ~209,500 on the mental/physical ground and ~63,500 schuldenbewind ([Divosa/Kennisbundel](https://www.kennisbundel.nl/kennisbank/artikel/onderzoek-naar-mensen-onder-beschermingsbewind/)).
- Older full-CBM snapshot (2018): ~350,000 protective measures total — ~250,000 bewind, ~75,000 mentorschap, ~22,000 curatele ([Schuldinfo](https://schuldinfo.nl/beschermingsbewind-curatele-mentorschap), [Pointer](https://pointer.kro-ncrv.nl/zorgen-om-forse-toename-bewind-en-mentorschap-in-de-zorg) — Pointer notes strong growth in bewind+mentorschap in care settings). ⚠️ No consolidated official 2024/2025 count was found; figures above are the best available and differ by source/definition.
- ~2,000+ professional CBM offices; ~300 were still non-digital when the 2026 mandate was announced (Rechtspraak news above).
- **Caseload norm:** courts: max **60–80 dossiers per fte** unaided (benchmark 70); funded time ~**17 h/dossier/yr** vs ~27 h actually needed per branch research (NBBI/Horus/NVVK survey of 400 bewindvoerders) — capacity strain is causing intake stops ([NBBI](https://www.nbbi.eu/bewindvoerders-gooien-de-deur-op-slot-voor-nieuwe-clienten/), [Kamerstuk 36464](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36464-2.html)).
- Branch organizations: **Horus** (branchevereniging wettelijk vertegenwoordigers), **NBBI**, **NVVK** (schuldhulp); accountancy side coordinated with **NBA**.

## Key recent-change flags (2024–2026)

1. **1-1-2026: electronic filing mandatory** for professionals (Aansluitpunt Toezicht / Mijn CBM).
2. **April 2025: LOVT Aanbevelingen consolidated**; machtiging threshold for goods/purpose-summed expenses now **€2,000** (was €1,500 — many secondary sources are outdated).
3. **2026 tariffs: +5.16% indexation + one-off +10.5%** (standard bewind €1,630, schuldenbewind €2,107, excl. BTW); pressure for further rises continues (NVVK 5-2026).
4. **CCBR register** renamed/extended (public schuldenbewind check per 1 April).
5. **1-7-2024: gewaarborgde hulp (PGB)** abolished, replaced by wettelijk vertegenwoordiger/gevolmachtigde (4-year transition) — LOVT B.C2.
6. Accountantsprotocol is reissued **annually** (2025 edition current at NBA) — compliance content shifts slightly each year.
7. Screenshot bank statements explicitly banned in R&V evidence (LOVT 2025) — bank-API/PDF-export integration is a real product requirement.

**Caveats:** rechtspraak.nl blocks automated fetches (403), so rechtspraak-page details were verified via search excerpts and the official LOVT PDF; the €95.90/hr 2026 uurtarief comes from sector tariff sheets rather than the consolidated regulation text and should be double-checked against Stcrt. 2025, 39037 before hard-coding it.
